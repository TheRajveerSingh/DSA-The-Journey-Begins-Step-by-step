A greedy algorithm basically says:
"At every step, make the best decision I can make right now, without worrying about changing that decision later."
While solving DSA, I'll store those problems here, which I've solved by greedy algorithm.

Such as file: G003_PlantingInEmptyFlowerbeds.cpp
Even though the problem was based on Arrays, Array describes the data structure. Greedy describes the problem-solving strategy.
Its to think about: What was the challenge while solving the problem?
Was it: "How do I access elements of an array?"
Or, was it: "Which empty positions should I choose to maximize the number of flowers I can plant?"
